November 11, 2026 Release

DayBoard 1.3.0

DayBoard 1.3.0 is the official release that follows the 1.3.0 betas. Compared with 1.2.0 it adds a Home page, mail you can triage, write and snooze, an Assistant beside every page, Quick Capture, a menu-bar agenda, Notes and tabs. It is also the first official release that is signed with DayBoard's Developer ID, notarized by Apple and able to update itself, and the first with DayBoard Pro.

It is the same code as 1.3.0-beta.22, with only the version number changed. The beta notes (beta.12 through beta.22) have the detail behind each item below.

New features

  • Home. DayBoard opens on a greeting and a one-line summary, the mail and follow-ups waiting on you, tasks suggested from your mail, and one Agenda card for Today or This week. Overdue items fold into a single row that remembers whether you left it open. The Agenda keeps its own sidebar row.
  • Mail you can work through. New mail is sorted into needs reply, FYI and ignorable, with a chip on every row. Inbox rows fit on one line, with Reply, Add task, Archive and Open in Gmail on hover (Settings → General → Inbox rows → With preview keeps the three-line rows). You can write and send mail with a 5-second Undo, snooze an email, ask to be reminded if nobody replies, and unsubscribe from newsletters in one click where the sender supports it.
  • Notifications and a Dock badge for snoozes that come back, unanswered follow-ups and, if you choose, new mail. Notifications start off. Both are in Settings → General → Mail.
  • The Assistant beside any page (⌥⌘J). It knows which email, event or to-do you have open. The composer has a model chip with a per-provider effort setting, / commands and @ mentions of your tasks, events and mail.
  • ⌘K. Jump to any view, email, event, to-do or note, or run an action.
  • Quick Capture. ⌃⌥⇧Space (changeable in Settings) opens a small panel over any app. Type a thought and press Enter, and it is filed in Google Tasks, Apple Reminders or Google Calendar, with Undo.
  • Menu-bar agenda. A menu-bar icon opens today's items, the next meeting, the mail that needs you, a collapsible overdue row and a capture box.
  • Up Next. A card for the next meeting with a Join button and AI meeting prep, and an optional notification before it starts. With a Granola MCP server set up, you can pull that meeting's notes afterwards and turn their action items into Tasks or Reminders.
  • Notes. Markdown notes with live preview, found with ⌘K. An Assistant chat can be saved as a note.
  • Memory. Tell DayBoard your preferences in Settings → AI. The Assistant and reply drafts follow them. A preference suggested from your edits is saved only when you pick Remember.
  • Tabs and your own sidebar. ⌘T opens a tab and your tabs come back next time. Reorder the sidebar views, hide any but Home, and turn off the count pills in Settings → General → Sidebar.
  • Keyboard. ⌘N opens New Item from the main views, J/K, Return, E, R, S and C work in the Inbox, and ? shows every shortcut.
  • A welcome for new installs. A four-step welcome covers what DayBoard is, connecting Google and Apple Reminders, choosing an AI tool (optional) and the shortcuts worth knowing. Every step can be skipped. Help → Welcome to DayBoard… opens it again.
  • Agent activity and usage. Settings → MCP Servers lists the DayBoard tools the Assistant or an outside agent used in the last 30 days (names and outcomes only). Settings → AI shows the AI requests and tokens DayBoard recorded per day and model.
  • Send feedback from the app. Help → Send Feedback… opens an email to support@getdayboard.com with your DayBoard and macOS versions filled in.

DayBoard Pro

  • Free, with no license check: Tasks, Gmail, Calendar, Reminders, sync and linking, notes, settings and the MCP server.
  • DayBoard Pro: the Assistant, AI actions, dictation, AI parsing in Quick Capture and learned memories. Each Mac gets a 14-day trial that starts the first time you use one of them. After the trial only AI locks. Nothing you wrote is lost, and everything else keeps working.
  • To unlock Pro, buy a key at getdayboard.com/pricing.html, open Settings → License, paste the key and click Activate. A key works on up to 3 Macs. Settings → License → Deactivate this device asks before it frees a seat. Your key appears on the receipt page after you pay and isn't emailed, so save it right away.
  • You still bring your own AI: Claude Code, Codex or an API key from your provider.

Install and updates

If updates never arrive, make sure DayBoard was dragged into Applications in Finder. A copy put there another way can run from a read-only location where macOS won't let it replace itself. Dragging it out of Applications and back in Finder once fixes this.

  • Signed, notarized and self-updating. The app and the disk image are signed with DayBoard's Developer ID and notarized by Apple. DayBoard checks for updates after launch and every few hours, downloads them in the background and shows Restart to update in the toolbar. If you don't restart, the update installs the next time you quit.
  • From 1.3.0-beta.12 or later, DayBoard updates itself to 1.3.0. Your data, sign-ins, trial and license stay where they are. Once you are on 1.3.0, DayBoard offers official releases only, not betas.
  • From anything older than 1.3.0-beta.12, download the DMG from getdayboard.com/download, quit DayBoard and drag the new app into Applications. App data lives outside the app and is kept.

Bug fixes

  • Going offline no longer signs you out of Google. DayBoard shows a connection error with Retry and keeps you signed in.
  • An open event editor no longer resets when the calendar refreshes in the background.
  • Note text survives a failed save, and a deleted note doesn't come back.
  • An email waiting in its Undo window is sent only from the Google account it was written in.
  • Saves and a normal quit no longer stall while the Google sign-in browser tab is open.
  • Google Calendar events that start and end at the same time show up in Agenda, Home and the menu bar, and no longer block planning slots.
  • MCP clients reach DayBoard after a port clash without a relaunch, and a turned-off MCP server no longer looks like a rejected key.
  • Long text stays in its row, Calendar "Focus on" works, and toasts are readable in dark mode.

Privacy and security

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. AI and MCP remain optional.

Questions: support@getdayboard.com.

  • An anonymous daily check-in. Once a day, DayBoard tells its license service at https://api.getdayboard.com that it's in use. It sends a random ID made only for this, the DayBoard version, and whether you're in the AI trial or have Pro. It never sends your calendar, tasks, reminders, mail, AI chats, Google account or license key. Settings → Updates → Send a daily anonymous check-in turns it off. The privacy policy has the details.
  • DayBoard also talks to the license service to activate or deactivate a key and for a daily key check. The trial is tracked on your Mac.
  • Memories are saved on your Mac. The activity log keeps tool names and outcomes, never what a tool read or wrote.
  • Electron 44.10.0 and updated bundled libraries, with the published security fixes listed in the beta.15 notes. The MCP SDK is 1.32.0, with input limits on DayBoard's local MCP servers.

Home, smarter mail, and an Assistant beside every page

This beta adds a Home page, mail you can send, snooze and clean up, an Assistant you can dock beside any view, and Notes. It carries everything planned for beta.16, which was tagged but never published: its release build stopped at a test before signing. Beta.17 was published on November 5, 2026 (UTC) from c9979977d065c41fac164aecb2cb9bc48fd8c718.

New features

  • Home. DayBoard opens on a greeting and a one-line summary, the mail and follow-ups waiting on you (oldest worries first, with how long each has waited), tasks suggested from your mail, your tasks and your meetings, for today or this week. The Agenda moves to its own sidebar row.

  • Write and send mail, with Undo for 5 seconds (change it in Settings → General → Mail). Reply drafts follow what you've asked DayBoard to remember.
  • Snooze and "remind me if no reply". A snoozed email leaves the inbox and comes back unread when you chose. A follow-up puts a thread back on Home if nobody answered.

Bug fixes

Privacy

  • Memories are saved on your Mac. The Assistant can't save one itself, and a preference suggested from your edits is saved only when you confirm it, so text inside an email can't plant one. Outside agents can add one, only while Allow changes is on.
  • The activity log keeps tool names and outcomes, never what a tool read or wrote.

DayBoard 1.3.0 beta 12

1.3.0-beta.12

DayBoard 1.3.0 beta 12 signed, self-updating, Quick Capture and the menu bar

This beta carries beta.11's Google sign-in forward and adds the native macOS work merged since then. It is a draft until the build is made and verified on an Apple Silicon Mac.

New features

  • DayBoard now updates itself. This is the first beta signed with DayBoard's Developer ID, notarized by Apple and published with update metadata. It checks for newer betas after launch and every few hours, downloads them in the background, and shows Restart to update in the toolbar when one is ready. If you don't restart, the update installs the next time you quit. Settings → Updates and DayBoard → Check for Updates… still check on request. Your data and sign-ins stay where they are.
  • Quick Capture. ⌃⌥⇧Space (changeable in Settings) opens a small panel over any app, including full-screen apps, on the display under the pointer. Type a thought and press Enter: the AI capture files it in Google Tasks, Apple Reminders or Google Calendar, focus goes straight back to the app you were in, and a confirmation offers Undo.
  • Menu-bar Agenda. A menu-bar icon opens today's items, the next meeting, recent captures and a capture box without bringing the whole dashboard forward.
  • Up Next. A card for the next meeting shows when it starts, a Join button and the AI meeting prep, with an optional notification shortly before it starts. If a Granola MCP server is set up in DayBoard, you can pull that meeting's notes afterwards and turn their action items into Tasks or Reminders. Each of these features can be switched off in Settings.
  • A smarter Assistant composer. The model chip has a per-provider effort setting, / opens commands and @ mentions your tasks, events and mail. Settings → AI and the composer share one model list.
  • ⌘N opens New Item from Agenda, Inbox, Calendar and the other main views, including when focus is on a row's action controls. It is ignored while a dialog or confirmation is open, so it never stacks a second New Item window. The + buttons show the shortcut in their tooltip.
  • The sidebar + is clickable everywhere. It was previously covered by the window drag strip in views where it was the only way to add an item. The empty title-bar strip still drags the window.
  • A more native window. Main pages share 24px headers and gutters and a stable history toolbar. Sidebar symbols use consistent 18px marks in 28px tiles, keeping the logo, provider identities, accent and density settings.
  • Responsive details pane. Editors and drafts stay mounted when the details pane moves between side, inline and stacked layouts. The pane can be resized and shown or hidden from the keyboard, and focus returns to the item you opened it from. Compact Calendar titles stay readable, and Calendar controls stay visible above the selected week.

Bug fixes

  • Going offline no longer signs you out of Google. If DayBoard renews its Google access while the Mac is offline or Google's sign-in service is failing, it now shows a connection error with Retry and keeps you signed in. Only a sign-in Google has actually revoked or expired asks you to sign in again.

Install and limits

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. Notarized, so it opens normally with no Open Anyway step.

Your data lives outside the bundle and stays. Because the signature changed, macOS may ask once whether DayBoard can use its saved key in your keychain; allow it so you stay signed in.
