Pricing

Free to use. $5 for AI.

DayBoard is free. DayBoard Pro is a one-time $5 purchase that unlocks its AI features. That's the launch price, which ends December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM PST. After that, Pro is $9. If you buy at $5, you keep your license and all future updates.

Free, for good

Everything that isn't AI stays free, with no time limit and no account to create:

  • The agenda with Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Apple Reminders and Gmail in one place.
  • Adding and changing events, tasks and reminders, and Gmail actions.
  • Linking duplicates so a reminder and a task show up once.
  • The menu bar, the local MCP server, settings and updates.

What DayBoard Pro adds

  • The Assistant, which answers questions and makes changes across your sources.
  • AI features such as the daily briefing and dictation.
  • Quick Capture's AI, which turns what you type into a task or reminder. Adding an item yourself stays free.

You still bring your own AI: Claude Code, Codex, or an API key from your provider. DayBoard Pro doesn't include AI usage. Your provider handles that under its own terms.

How it works

  • One payment. $5 once, with no subscription.
  • Future updates included. Pro keeps working as DayBoard updates.
  • Up to 3 Macs. To move your license, open Settings → License on the old Mac and choose Deactivate this device.
  • Try AI first. You get 14 days of AI free, starting the first time you use an AI feature. When the trial ends, only AI locks; everything else keeps working.
  • 14-day refunds. Not for you? Get a full refund within 14 days. See the refund policy.

Paying

Checkout is run by Stripe. Link, Stripe's checkout service, is the seller of record: it processes your payment, calculates any sales tax or VAT, and emails your receipt. Your statement shows LINK.COM* DAYBOARD. DayBoard never sees your card number. Your license key appears on the receipt page after you pay. Save it right away: the receipt page works only in the browser you paid with, and only for 7 days. See the privacy policy for what each side keeps.

Open source

DayBoard's source is licensed under GPL-3.0, so you can always build it yourself. Buying Pro funds development and doesn't change your rights under the license.
