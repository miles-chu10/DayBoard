Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions, and the fastest way to report a problem.

How do I get DayBoard?

DayBoard is a free download. The app is notarized by Apple and updates itself. Google hasn't verified DayBoard yet, so connecting a Google account shows Google's "unverified app" warning, and Google limits how many accounts can connect until its review is done.

macOS won't open DayBoard. What do I do?

The current app and its disk image are signed and notarized. The download page has the install steps. If macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet, click Open. If it blocks the download or app, contact the DayBoard team with the warning text and your macOS version. On a build before 1.3.0 beta 12, install the current version using the install steps.

Which Macs are supported?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs aren't supported yet.

Do I need all four services?

No. Turn on only the sources you use in Settings → Sources. Google services need a Google sign-in, and Apple Reminders asks for macOS permission.

Does DayBoard require an AI account?

No. AI is optional and off until you choose a provider. If you turn it on, only the context each request needs is sent to that provider.

Is my data sent to DayBoard?

No. DayBoard talks directly to Google and to Apple Reminders on your Mac. There's no DayBoard server that sees your calendar, tasks, reminders or mail. See where your data goes.

Will DayBoard stay free?

Yes. DayBoard is free. Its AI features need DayBoard Pro: $5 once, after a 14-day AI trial, with a 14-day refund. See pricing. The source stays open under GPL-3.0, so you can always build it yourself.

I joined the waitlist. How do I remove my address?

Email the DayBoard team and we'll delete your address. See the privacy policy for what we store.

Report a problem

Tell us what broke.

Email support@getdayboard.com with what you expected, what happened, and your DayBoard and macOS versions. Please leave out passwords, account tokens and private mail. Prefer GitHub? Open an issue.
