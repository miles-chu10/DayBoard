# DayBoard 1.3.12 — A clearer License page

DayBoard 1.3.12 rebuilds **Settings → License** so you can see your plan at a glance, tidies the
places the app shows DayBoard Pro, and fixes the account line in the sidebar.

## New

- **A new License page.** A plan card on top shows your plan and its status: Active, days left in
  the AI trial with a progress bar, or what needs attention. Below it, **This Mac** lists the saved
  key ending, the activation state and how many Macs a key covers, and **Included with Pro** lists
  what you get (#174).
- **Deactivate this device** is now a small button with a line that explains what it does, instead
  of a button across the whole page. It still asks before it does anything (#174).
- **License help** emails support or copies the address (#174).
- The DayBoard Pro card in Settings and in the sidebar has a clearer layout with the price and what
  is included, the screen you see once the AI trial has ended is tidier, and a small **Pro** badge
  sits beside your name once DayBoard Pro is active (#174).

## Fixed

- The line under your name in the sidebar no longer says **Connected** when Google needs you to
  sign in again. It now says **Google needs sign-in**, or **Google needs permission** when Tasks or
  Calendar wasn't allowed on Google's screen.

## Updating

- Nothing changes in how you buy, activate or deactivate DayBoard Pro. Pro is $5 until December 31,
  2026, then $9.
- **Gmail needs an app password since 1.3.10.** If your inbox shows **Connect Gmail**, open
  **Settings → Sources → Gmail → Connect Gmail**, make an app password in your Google account and
  paste it in.
- Your data, sign-ins, trial and license stay where they are.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. AI and MCP remain optional.

Questions: support@getdayboard.com.
