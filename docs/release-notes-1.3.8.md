# DayBoard 1.3.8 — Your to-dos and settings menu stay in the menu bar

DayBoard 1.3.8 keeps more of your day inside the menu-bar panel, so you can check things off without
opening the main window.

## New

- The panel's third button now lists every open to-do right in the panel, grouped by Google Tasks and
  Apple Reminders list. Tick one to complete it, fold a list away, or press **+** on a list to aim
  quick add at it (#150).
- The gear opens a menu inside the panel with **Sync** (⌘R), **Settings…** (⌘,), **Open Main
  Window** (⌘O), **Keyboard shortcuts** and **Quit DayBoard** (⌘Q). The shortcuts work while the
  panel is open (#150).

## Updating

- **From 1.3.2,** DayBoard shows the Software Update window with these notes before it installs.
- Your data, sign-ins, trial and license stay where they are.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. AI and MCP remain optional.
