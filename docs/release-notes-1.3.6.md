# DayBoard 1.3.6 — A Home you can arrange and a faster menu bar

DayBoard 1.3.6 lets you arrange the cards on Home, reworks the menu-bar panel so adding a to-do
to a specific list takes one line, and keeps your email drafts safer.

## New

- **Arrange Home your way.** Choose **Customize** on Home to reorder the cards by dragging or with
  the move buttons, hide the ones you don't use, collapse a card, and set a card to half or full
  width. **Reset layout** puts everything back (#135).
- **A clearer menu-bar panel.** Each day's events sit in one card with their start and end times,
  and the card folds to a count. Sections show how many items they hold, Overdue follows Today,
  and to-dos show their due time, in red once they are late. The tabs along the bottom are icons
  (#134).
- **Add to a list from the menu bar.** Quick add now says which list it adds to. Pick any Google
  Tasks or Apple Reminders list, or **Let AI decide** while AI capture is on. **Add with
  details…** opens the full capture form (#134).

## Fixed

- An email you are writing is kept when Undo or Edit brings back an older one. The older email
  opens after you finish or close the current one (#133).
- Quitting while an email is still waiting to send now asks first. **Keep Open** leaves the email's
  Undo time alone. DayBoard does not ask while your Mac shuts down, restarts or logs out (#133).
- A note changed somewhere else is no longer overwritten by an older copy. DayBoard offers to
  keep your version as a separate note (#133).
- After your Mac comes back online, snoozes, follow-ups and the license check retry within five
  minutes (#133).
- Clicking the Dock icon, or choosing File → New Tab, brings the main window back when only
  Settings is open (#138).

## Updating

- **From 1.3.2,** DayBoard shows the Software Update window with these notes before it installs.
- Your data, sign-ins, trial and license stay where they are.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. AI and MCP remain optional.
